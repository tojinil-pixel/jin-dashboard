Drive Uploader

Drive Uploader is a private tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. The owner uses it to upload videos they made to their own YouTube channels and to set each video's details: title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist, captions, scheduled publishing time and a first comment. It can also read statistics about the owner's own videos and work with files in the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets.

It is not a public service. No one else can sign up for it or use it, and it has no server.
